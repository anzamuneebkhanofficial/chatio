# Bella Vista Restaurant — Knowledge Base

## About the Restaurant

Bella Vista is a fine dining Italian restaurant located in the heart of downtown. We have been serving authentic Italian cuisine since 2008. Our restaurant seats up to 120 guests and features a private dining room for events up to 30 people.

## Reservations

Reservations are strongly recommended, especially on weekends. Book online via our website or call us directly. Walk-ins are welcome based on availability.

Group bookings of 8 or more require a minimum 48 hours advance notice.

## Dietary Options

We accommodate most dietary requirements including vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, and dairy-free. Please inform our staff of any allergies when booking or on arrival.

## Private Events

Our private dining room is available for birthdays, anniversaries, corporate dinners, and other events. Minimum spend applies. Contact us at events@bellavista.com for availability and custom menus.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Do you have parking?
A: Yes, complimentary valet parking is available Thursday through Sunday from 6:00 PM.

Q: Do you accept walk-ins?
A: Yes, we welcome walk-ins based on availability. Reservations are recommended to guarantee your table.

Q: Is there a dress code?
A: Smart casual attire is recommended. We ask that guests refrain from wearing sportswear.

Q: Do you offer takeaway?
A: Yes, takeaway is available for our full menu. Orders can be placed by phone or through our website.

Q: What is your cancellation policy?
A: Reservations can be cancelled up to 24 hours in advance without charge. Late cancellations or no-shows may incur a fee.
