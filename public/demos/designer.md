## Process

1. Discovery Call — We discuss your goals, brand vision, target audience, and project scope.
2. Proposal — You receive a detailed proposal with pricing, timeline, and deliverables.
3. Design — Work begins with initial concepts sent for your review.
4. Revisions — We refine based on your feedback.
5. Final Delivery — All files delivered in agreed formats via email or Google Drive.
