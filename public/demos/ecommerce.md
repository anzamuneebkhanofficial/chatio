## Returns and Refunds

We offer a 30-day return policy on all products.
Items must be in original, unopened condition with all accessories and packaging.
Opened software, digital downloads, and customized orders are non-refundable.

To start a return: visit our Returns Center at techcart.com/returns or contact support.
Refunds are processed within 5 to 7 business days after we receive the returned item.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I cancel an order?
A: Orders can be cancelled within 1 hour of placement. After that, the order may already be processed for shipping.

Q: Do you price match?
A: Yes, we match prices from major authorized retailers including Amazon, Best Buy, and B&H Photo. Contact support with a link to the lower price.

Q: Is my payment information secure?
A: Yes, all transactions are encrypted with 256-bit SSL. We never store full card numbers.

Q: Do you sell refurbished products?
A: Yes, we have a certified refurbished section with products graded A (like new) or B (good condition). All refurbished items are tested and come with a 90-day warranty.

Q: How do I contact customer support?
A: Via live chat on our website, email at support@techcart.com, or phone at 1-800-555-9900.
